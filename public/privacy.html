Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Building Innovation AI ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application and website.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways. The information we may collect includes:

  • Photos and Camera: Our app requires access to your device's camera and photo gallery to function. Images you upload are sent to our secure servers to be processed by our Artificial Intelligence (AI) algorithms to generate architectural and interior design visualizations.
  • Personal Data: Personally identifiable information, such as your name and email address that you voluntarily give to us when you register an account.
  • Analytics and Device Data: We collect anonymous usage data, IP addresses, and crash reports to improve app performance.

2. Data Collection and Usage

  • AI Processing: The images you provide are strictly used for generating the requested room visualizations. We do not use your personal images to train our AI models without explicit consent.
  • Account Management: To process your transactions via secure payment gateways (e.g., Stripe) and maintain your saved generated designs.

3. Data Sharing and Security

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data or uploaded images with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. All data transmission is secured using industry-standard encryption (HTTPS).

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Images processed by our AI are stored temporarily to deliver the result to your device and are then deleted from our active processing servers. You have the right to request the deletion of your account and all associated data at any time through the app's settings or by contacting our support team.

5. Contact Us
