Google Play Data Safety Section Details:
1. Data Collected:
   - Photos & Videos (Photos): Collected for app functionality (AI room visualization & material estimation). Encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Ephemeral / deleted after processing or stored in user project gallery until user requests deletion.
   - Personal Info (Name, Email Address): Collected for account management and authentication.
   - Financial Info (Purchase History): Collected for subscription and quota management.
   - App Info & Performance (Crash logs, Diagnostics): Collected for analytics and app stability.
2. Data Sharing:
   - No data shared with 3rd-party advertisers or data brokers.
   - Photos transmitted securely over HTTPS to Google Gemini Vision API strictly for user-requested image rendering.
3. Security & Deletion:
   - Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3).
   - Users can request data and account deletion in-app and via web URL: https://buildingapp.us/account-deletion
